Equal ice time for everyone.

For youth hockey coaches. HockeySub tells you when it is time to change lines, shows who goes on and who comes off, and keeps track of playing time. So you can watch the game instead of the clock.

Everything you need on the bench

Made for the coach standing with the kids, not with a spreadsheet.

Before the game

Add your players, pick an age group and tick who is here today. Players on the ice, game length and shift length are filled in from the youth rules, and you can change everything afterwards.

  • Ready-made setups for Norway, Sweden, IIHF and Canada
  • Choose the starting skaters and the goalie
  • Try a demo game before your first real one

Time to change

The app tells you with a vibration and a sound when the shift is over, and shows who comes off and who goes on. Tap “Change now”, and playing time keeps counting for every player.

  • Fair rotation, so everyone gets about the same ice time
  • Tap a player to choose who changes next
  • Injured player? Bring someone in from the bench with one tap

From the lock screen

The clock and the next change show on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. You can tap “Change now” without unlocking your phone, and watch the game instead of the screen.

After the game

Every game is saved in the archive with each player’s playing time, and with the result when the score counter is on. You can correct a game afterwards.

  • Share a tidy game report as a PDF with parents or the club
  • Pro: season stats with charts, also as a PDF
  • Pro: several teams and iCloud sharing with assistant coaches
  • Pro: a points table with goals and assists

How much ice time does each child get?

Pick an age group and how many skaters you have, and see how much ice time each child gets when the time is shared equally. That is what HockeySub keeps track of for you during the game.

Ready in a minute

Three steps from download to your first line change.

Add your players

Type in their names, with jersey numbers if you like, and choose the goalie.

Pick age group and who’s playing

The rules are filled in for the age group. Tick off who is here today.

Start the game

When the app tells you, tap the change button. After the game, everything is in the archive.

See the app

Calm colours, big buttons and only what you need during a game. Scroll sideways to see more.

Your players stay on your phone

No account, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone, and is only shared when you choose to.
