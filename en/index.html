For youth hockey coaches

Equal ice time for everyone.

HockeySub tells you when it is time to change lines, shows who goes on and who comes off, and keeps track of playing time. So you can watch the game instead of the clock.

Coming soon to the App Store See what it does

Free to download. For iPhone, no account and no ads. In English, Norwegian and Swedish.

Everything you need on the bench

Made for the coach standing with the kids, not with a spreadsheet.

Shift timer

The app tells you with a vibration and a sound when the shift is over, and shows who goes on and who comes off.

Equal ice time

Playing time is counted for every player, and fair rotation makes sure everyone gets about the same time on the ice.

Change lines from the lock screen

The clock and the next change show on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, so you don’t have to unlock your phone.

Norwegian league rules

Pick an age group from U7 to U13, and the number of players, game length and shift length are filled in from the Norwegian Ice Hockey Federation (NIHF) rules. You can change everything afterwards.

You decide

Tap a player to choose who changes next. Injured player? Bring someone in from the bench with one tap.

Archive

Every game is saved with the result and each player’s playing time. You can correct a game afterwards.

Pro

Season stats

See each player’s average over the whole season, or for the dates you choose. Share the stats as a PDF.

Pro

Share as PDF

Create a tidy game report and send it to parents or the club. One game is free. With Pro you can put several games together in one PDF.

Several teams

Keep several teams in one app, each with its own roster, game setup and archive. Switch teams with one tap.

iCloud sharing with assistant coaches

Invite assistant coaches via iCloud, and the roster and archive stay in sync for everyone automatically. Assistant coaches join for free, and sending the team as a file is always free.

Ready in a minute

Three steps from download to your first line change.

Add your players

Type in their names, with jersey numbers if you like, and choose the goalie.

Pick age group and who’s playing

The rules are filled in for the age group. Tick off who is here today.

Start the game

When the app tells you, tap the change button. After the game, everything is in the archive.

See the app

Calm colours, big buttons and only what you need during a game. Scroll sideways to see more.

Your players stay on your phone

No account, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone, and is only shared when you choose to.
