User guide

1. Getting started

The first time you open HockeySub, you get a short three-page introduction. There you can also tap Prøv en demo-kamp (try a demo game) to see how a game works with a ready-made demo team. The demo game is not saved and doesn’t touch your team.

The setup screen

After the introduction you land on Oppsett (setup). This is where you do everything before the game:

  • Neste kamp (next game, the blue card at the top) shows the opponent, how many players are here, game length and time per shift. Tap the card to open the game settings.
  • Kampoppsett (game settings) holds the rules for the game: age group, game length, line changes and goalie.
  • Startende utespillere (starting skaters) is who goes on the ice first.
  • Spillerstall (roster) is your team, and who is here today.
  • Start kamp (start game) is always at the bottom. If something is missing, the text above the button tells you what to do.

The buttons at the top

  • The archive box on the left opens Arkiv (archive) with all saved games.
  • The question mark has the introduction (Slik fungerer appen, how the app works), the demo game and a link to this guide (Brukermanual, user guide) and HockeySub Pro.
  • The team button (two people) switches between your teams and opens Administrer lag (manage teams).

Ready in a minute: add your players, pick an age group under Kampoppsett, tick off who is here and tap Start kamp. You can fine-tune the rest later.

2. The roster

Adding players

  1. Type the name in the Ny spiller (new player) field.
  2. Tap Legg til (add), or Done on the keyboard.
  3. Repeat for the whole team. The goalie is added like any other player.

Jersey numbers, names and deleting

Tap … next to a player to choose Legg til draktnummer (add jersey number), Endre navn (rename) or Slett (delete). The jersey number is shown in front of the name during the game and in the archive, for example “#8 Jakob”. Leave the field empty to remove the number.

Who is here today?

Tap the circle in front of a name to tick off who is here. Only ticked players take part in the game. The … menu at the top of the roster has Alle til stede (everyone here), which ticks everyone at once.

The order

Players go on the ice in the order they appear in the roster. Choose Endre rekkefølge (change order) under … at the top of the roster and drag players up or down. Tap Done when you’re happy.

Who starts?

Tap Startende utespillere (starting skaters) on the setup screen and tick the players who start on the ice. The goalie comes in addition. If you choose fewer than there is room for, the rest are filled in following the roster order. Tøm (clear) removes your choices.

3. Game settings

Tap Kampoppsett (game settings) on the setup screen, or the blue card. Everything is saved right away, so just tap Ferdig (done) when you’re ready.

Age group and Norwegian rules

Pick an age group at the top under Klasse (age group), and the rules from the Norwegian Ice Hockey Federation (NIHF) are filled in for you. You can change the values afterwards. The age group then shows as Egendefinert (custom).

Kampregler (game rules) has an easy-to-read summary of the rules for every age group, in Norwegian, with links to the official NIHF documents. The summary is stored in the app, so you can read it at the rink without a connection.

U7–U9: Turn on Stopp klokka ved bytte (stop the clock at line changes) so the app follows stop time. U13: The app doesn’t have periods yet, so the game is set to 40 minutes.

Kamp (game)

  • Motstander (opponent): the name is shown during the game, in the archive and in the PDF report.
  • Kamplengde (game length): from 5 to 120 minutes.

Bytter (line changes)

  • Tid mellom bytter (time between changes): how long each shift lasts, from 20 seconds to 3 minutes.
  • Spillere på isen (players on the ice): how many play at the same time, including the goalie.
  • Byttes samtidig (changed at once): how many go off and on at each change. Usually the whole line. If you change fewer than the whole line, the app shows a warning, since NIHF rules say no one should play two shifts in a row.

Målvakt (goalie)

With Fast målvakt (fixed goalie) turned on, you choose who plays in goal. The goalie plays the whole game and is not part of the rotation. Turn it off and everyone rotates.

Under kampen (during the game)

  • Spør før bytte (ask before changing): the app shows Tid for bytte! (time to change) and waits for you to tap Bytt nå (change now). When off, the app changes lines by itself when time is up.
  • Stopp klokka ved bytte (stop the clock at line changes): the game clock stops when time is up and starts again when you tap Bytt nå.
  • Lyd ved bytte (sound at line changes): a short sound in addition to the vibration.
  • Rettferdig rotasjon (fair rotation): the players with the least ice time go on, and those with the most come off. When off, players change in a fixed order.

4. During the game

Tap Start kamp (start game). The screen stays awake as long as the clock is running.

The top of the screen

  • The ring counts down to the next change. It turns amber in the last ten seconds.
  • The game clock shows the time left. If the game runs over, the extra time is shown with a plus sign.
  • The score: tap + next to Vi (us) or the opponent when someone scores. − fixes a mistaken tap.
  • Ut (off) and Inn (on) show who changes next.

The lists

Below the top you see Målvakt (goalie), Utespillere på is (skaters on the ice), Benken (the bench) and, if needed, Ute av kampen (out of the game). The time next to each player is their total ice time. Small arrows show who goes off and on at the next change.

Time to change

When time is up, the phone vibrates and Tid for bytte! (time to change) appears with who goes off and on. Tap Bytt nå (change now). If you need to wait a moment, tap Ikke nå (not now). The ring then says Bytt! (change) and shows how long the change has been waiting. Tap the ring to bring the prompt back.

You can tap the Bytt nå button at the bottom at any time, also before time is up. The number on the button is how many players change. The pause button next to it stops and starts the clock.

Choosing who goes on and off

Tap a player to see the options:

  • On the ice: Bytt ut nå – velg hvem som går inn (change now, choose who goes on), Ut ved neste bytte (off at next change), Skadet – bytt inn fra benken (injured, bring in from the bench) and Fjern fra kampen (remove from game).
  • On the bench: Bytt inn nå – velg hvem som går ut (change now, choose who comes off), Inn ved neste bytte (on at next change) and Fjern fra kampen (remove from game).

Players you’ve picked for the next change get a small pin. Tap the player again and choose Ikke ut/inn ved neste bytte (not off/on at next change) to undo.

Injuries or a player who has to leave

Skadet – bytt inn fra benken (injured) takes the player off the ice and puts the next player from the bench on right away. Fjern fra kampen (remove from game) moves the player to Ute av kampen (out of the game). When the player is ready again, tap Ta inn (bring back).

Ending the game

Tap … at the top right and choose Stopp og lagre kampen (stop and save the game). The game is saved in the archive. If it was a practice game you don’t want to keep, choose Stopp og forkast kampen (stop and discard the game).

Tilbake (back) at the top left takes you to the setup screen without stopping the game. Tap Fortsett kamp (continue game) to return. If the app is closed in the middle of a game, it picks up where it left off when you open it again.

5. Lock screen, Dynamic Island and Apple Watch

While a game is running, HockeySub shows a Live Activity on the lock screen. It shows the countdown to the next change, who goes on, who comes after them, and the score. You can tap Bytt nå (change now) right from the lock screen without opening the app.

On an iPhone with Dynamic Island, the countdown stays at the top of the screen while you use other apps. Touch and hold it to see more and get the Bytt nå button.

If you have an Apple Watch, the same information appears in the Smart Stack on your watch, with its own Bytt nå button.

Can’t see it? Go to Settings > HockeySub on your iPhone and check that Live Activities is turned on.

6. Archive and season stats

Tap the archive box at the top left of the setup screen. At the top you see wins, draws, losses and goal difference for all games. Below are the games, grouped by month.

Game details

Tap a game to see the result, the settings and how long each player was on the ice.

Correcting a game

Tap Rediger (edit) in the game details. You can change the opponent, goals for and against, and each player’s ice time (tap the player). Tap Lagre (save).

Sharing a game as a PDF

Tap the share button at the top right of the game details (Del som PDF, share as PDF). Send the report via Messages, email or AirDrop, or save it to Files.

Sharing several games

  1. Tap … at the top right of the archive and choose Velg kamper å dele (choose games to share).
  2. Tap the games you want, or Velg alle (select all).
  3. Tap Del … kamper (share … games) at the bottom. You get one PDF with one page per game.

Pro: Sharing several games at once needs HockeySub Pro. You can always share one game at a time for free.

Deleting games

Swipe a game to the left and tap Slett (delete). Under … you can also choose Slett kamper (delete games) to delete several, or Slett alle (delete all). Deleting can’t be undone.

Season stats

Tap Sesongstatistikk (season stats) at the top of the archive. It shows each player’s ice time across all games. The bar shows the average per game, so it’s fair even if someone has played fewer games. Games played as goalie are not counted in the average.

Under Periode (period), choose Alle (all), Denne sesongen (this season, from 1 August) or Velg (choose) for your own dates. The stats can also be shared as a PDF with the share button at the top.

Pro: Season stats, including the PDF, are part of HockeySub Pro. The archive, game details and correcting games are free.

7. Several teams and sharing with an assistant coach

Several teams

Tap the team button at the top right of the setup screen and choose Administrer lag (manage teams). Under Nytt lag (new team), type the name and tap Legg til (add). Each team has its own roster, game settings and archive.

Once you have several teams, switch between them with the team button. The team name is shown at the top of the setup screen. You can’t switch teams while a game is running.

Tap the pencil to rename a team, or swipe left to delete it. The app tells you how many players and games will be lost before you delete.

Pro: Your first team is free. To create a second team and more, you need HockeySub Pro. Teams you get from an assistant coach or import from a file don’t need Pro.

Sharing your team with an assistant coach

  1. Open Administrer lag (manage teams) and go to Del med medtrener (share with assistant coach).
  2. Tap Del laget (share team), choose Send som fil (send as file) and send the file via AirDrop, Messages or email.
  3. The assistant coach taps the file, chooses HockeySub and then Hent laget (get team).

The roster, archive and game settings are included. If the assistant coach already has the team, the button says Oppdater laget (update team) instead. New players and games are then added, and nothing is deleted. If you received the file some other way, use Hent lag fra fil (get team from file).

If you’d rather keep the team in sync automatically, choose Inviter via iCloud (invite via iCloud) instead of Send som fil, and send the link to your assistant coach. From then on the roster and archive sync between you.

Pro: Inviter via iCloud needs HockeySub Pro, but only for you as the one who invites. The assistant coach joins for free. Send som fil is always free.

8. HockeySub Pro

HockeySub is free to download, and everything you need during a game is free. HockeySub Pro is a one-time purchase of 79 kr (NOK). It is not a subscription.

What Pro unlocks

  • Several teams: a second team and more. Teams you get from an assistant coach or import from a file don’t need Pro.
  • Season stats, including as a PDF.
  • Sharing several games at once as one PDF.
  • iCloud sync with assistant coaches: only the coach who invites needs Pro. The assistant coach joins for free.

What’s free

Line changes and Bytt nå (change now), the lock screen and Dynamic Island, fair rotation and choosing who goes on. Norwegian (NIHF) setup and game rules. One team, the archive and correcting games. Sharing one game as a PDF and sending the team as a file. The introduction and the demo game.

How to buy

In the app, Pro features have a small PRO badge or a lock. Tap one of them to open the Pro screen. You can also find it under the question mark on the setup screen, as HockeySub Pro. Tap Kjøp Pro for 79 kr (buy Pro for 79 kr) and confirm with your Apple ID.

New phone or deleted the app?

Open the Pro screen and tap Gjenopprett kjøp (restore purchase). Use the same Apple ID you bought with. You won’t pay again.

Family Sharing

Pro supports Family Sharing. If you have purchase sharing turned on, the rest of your family can use Pro without buying it themselves.

The purchase is handled by Apple in the App Store. We don’t receive any payment information.

9. Frequently asked questions

How does the app make sure everyone gets equal ice time?

The app counts each player’s ice time and shows who goes off and on. Turn on Rettferdig rotasjon (fair rotation) under Kampoppsett, and the players with the least ice time always go on, while those with the most come off. After the game you can see the split in the archive and season stats.

A player arrived late. What do I do?

Players can’t be added once the game has started. If you’re waiting for someone, tick them as here before you start. Then tap the player during the game and choose Fjern fra kampen (remove from game). When the player arrives, tap Ta inn (bring back) under Ute av kampen (out of the game).

I don’t get a sound or vibration when it’s time to change.

Check that Lyd ved bytte (sound at line changes) is on under Kampoppsett, that the phone isn’t on silent, and that notifications are allowed for HockeySub under Settings > Notifications.

Why can’t I tap Start kamp?

The text above the button tells you what’s missing. Usually there are too few players here, or Fast målvakt (fixed goalie) is on without a goalie selected.

What happens if I start a new game while one is running?

The app asks whether you want to save or discard the running game before the new one starts.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is stored only on your phone, and is only shared when you choose to. Read more in the privacy policy.
