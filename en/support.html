Getting started

The app is currently in Norwegian. Button names are shown in Norwegian below, with the English meaning in brackets.

  1. Add your players to the roster, with jersey numbers if you like, and choose the goalie.
  2. Open Kampoppsett (game settings) and pick an age group. The Norwegian (NIHF) rules are filled in, and you can change them afterwards.
  3. Tick off who is here today, and choose who starts.
  4. Tap Start kamp (start game). When it’s time to change lines, the app tells you and shows who goes on and off. Tap Bytt nå (change now).
  5. When the game is over, tap Stopp og lagre kampen (stop and save the game). It is then saved in Arkiv (archive).

Want to try it first? Choose Prøv en demo-kamp (try a demo game) in the introduction, or under the question mark on the setup screen. The demo game is not saved.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose who changes myself?

Tap a player during the game. You can then choose Inn ved neste bytte (on at next change) or Ut ved neste bytte (off at next change), or change right away. Otherwise the app decides: with Rettferdig rotasjon (fair rotation), the player with the least playing time goes on and the one with the most comes off.

A player got injured. What do I do?

Tap the player on the ice and choose Skadet – bytt inn fra benken (injured, bring in from the bench). The next player on the bench goes on right away. If the player won’t play any more, choose Fjern fra kampen (remove from game). The player moves to Ute av kampen (out of the game) and can be brought back with Ta inn (bring back).

Can I change lines without unlocking my phone?

Yes. During a game, the clock and the next change show on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, with its own Bytt nå button. If you don’t see it, check that Live Activities are turned on for HockeySub in the iPhone Settings app.

I get no sound or vibration when it’s time to change.

Check that Lyd ved bytte (sound at change) is on in game settings, that your phone is not on silent, and that notifications are allowed for HockeySub in Settings > Notifications.

Should the clock stop when it’s time to change?

You decide. With Stopp klokka ved bytte (stop the clock at change), the clock stands still until you tap Bytt nå, as in Norwegian U7–U9 games with stop time. Otherwise the clock keeps running during the change.

What happens if the app closes during a game?

The game continues where it left off when you open the app again.

Can I correct a game afterwards?

Yes. Open the game in Arkiv (archive) and choose Rediger (edit). There you can change goals and each player’s playing time.

How do I share games as a PDF?

Open a game in Arkiv and choose Del som PDF (share as PDF). To share several games at once, choose Velg kamper å dele (choose games to share) in the archive menu. Season stats can also be shared as a PDF. One game is free, while several games at once and season stats need HockeySub Pro.

How do I share my team with an assistant coach?

Tap the team button at the top of the setup screen, choose Administrer lag (manage teams) and go to Del med medtrener (share with assistant coach). Tap Del laget (share team), choose Inviter via iCloud (invite via iCloud) and send the link in Messages. When the assistant coach taps the link, the team appears in the app, and from then on the roster and archive sync automatically between you. Attendance and game settings stay your own. Under Hvem som har tilgang (who has access) you can see who the team is shared with and stop sharing. If your assistant coach doesn’t use iCloud, choose Send som fil (send as file) instead. The roster, archive and game settings are then sent as a file the assistant coach opens in HockeySub. Inviter via iCloud needs HockeySub Pro for the coach who invites. Send som fil is free.

Can I have several teams?

Yes. Tap the team button at the top of the setup screen to switch teams, or choose Administrer lag to add, rename or delete teams. Each team has its own roster, game settings and archive. Your first team is free. A second team and more need HockeySub Pro.

What is HockeySub Pro?

A one-time purchase of 79 kr (NOK), with no subscription. Pro gives you several teams, season stats, sharing several games as one PDF and iCloud sync with assistant coaches. Everything you need during a game is free. Pro supports Family Sharing. Read more in the user guide.

I bought Pro, but it’s locked on my new phone.

Open the Pro screen, for example from the question mark on the setup screen, and tap Gjenopprett kjøp (restore purchase). Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. You won’t pay again.

Does my assistant coach need to buy Pro too?

No. Only you, as the one who invites with Inviter via iCloud (invite via iCloud), need Pro. The assistant coach joins for free. Send som fil (send as file) is free for everyone.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is stored only on your phone. Read more in the privacy policy.
